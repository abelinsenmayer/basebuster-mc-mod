Modrinth link: https://modrinth.com/mod/base-buster

Base Buster improves on vanilla raid mechanics. Instead of being centered around villages, raiders now come to your base. If the raiders capture your flag, you lose!

This mod is early in development. Current features:
- Designate and name your territory using a "base flag".
- Vanilla raids are disabled. Instead, raids periodically come to your base! Defeat the boss raider(s), and the rest will flee.
- Raiders tunnel through barriers and build scaffolding to cross gaps or scale walls. Any mob griefing within your base flag's claimed area can be quickly and automatically repaired by using a repair hammer on your flag.
- Summon a raid at your base (or another player's) using the Bad Omen effect.
- Victory banners gained from raids, which you can place near your flag to level it up, inviting more challenging raids.

Planned features:
- Additional rewards for fending off raids.
- Improved aesthetic cohesion, and the removal of vanilla raids.

I develop this project entirely in my spare time. I make no promises to update, maintain, or fix anything in it.

Hope you enjoy!
